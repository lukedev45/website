Einstein was frustrated with the state of quantum mechanics; *wave function* this and *uncertatinty principle* that.
But he was especially conflicted with the idea that he coined "spooky action at a distance".
Locality is a very inuitive and clear notion in reality, events are separated by cause and effect.
Imainge a set of dominos standing up right and they are all arranged in a line, it is very clear that tipping the first domino will result in a cascade of dominos falling one after the other.
It, usually, never occurs in reality that psuhish one domino will simuntaneously and immediately control another domino.
Or imagine a case with a Voodoo doll. If you were unlucky enough for someone to curse you and put part of your soul into a voodoo doll then the person controlling the voodoo could inflict pain and misery into your life at a moments notice with **no delay**.
In modern day physics we call this "non-locality" and "locality" is the opposite of this.
