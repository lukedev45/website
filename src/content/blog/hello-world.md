Welcome to the first post. I built this site as a small, quiet corner of the
internet to write down what I'm learning and to keep a record of the things I
build.

I expect most posts to fall into a few buckets:

- **Notes** — short write-ups of something I figured out.
- **Projects** — longer pieces about things I've made.
- **Ideas** — the occasional opinion about software and design.

No newsletter, no pop-ups, no tracking. Just words. Thanks for reading.
