---
title: 'On minimalism'
description: 'Why a smaller surface area is usually the better default.'
pubDate: 2026-06-02
tags: ['writing', 'design']
---

Minimalism isn't about having less for its own sake. It's about making the
important things easy to find and the unimportant things easy to ignore.

A page with one column, readable type, and a clear hierarchy asks almost nothing
of the reader. Every extra widget, sidebar, and animation is a small tax on
attention. Most of them aren't worth it.

The same is true of code. The cheapest feature to maintain is the one you didn't
build. When in doubt, leave it out — you can always add it back when there's a
real reason to.
